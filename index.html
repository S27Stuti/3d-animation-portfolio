The Designer

Hi, I'm Stuti Shah.

I am a passionate 3D artist, UI/UX designer, and frontend developer dedicated to transforming creative concepts into tangible, high-fidelity visual experiences.

With extensive hands-on experience in Blender for 3D modeling, texturing, studio lighting, and keyframe animation, I combine artistic vision with technical precision. Complementing my 3D expertise, I design user-centric interfaces in Figma and develop responsive web interfaces using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and the MERN stack.

Whether crafting photorealistic architectural spaces, stylized vehicle concepts, or interactive WebGL web journeys, I aim to create work that captivates and performs seamlessly across mobile, tablet, and desktop screens.
